Interest and charges

  • Interest on unpaid balances: 3.5% a month (42% a year), from the transaction date.
  • Interest-free period: up to 48 days if the full balance is paid by the due date.
  • Late payment fee: ₹0 to ₹1,300 by the amount due. Goods and Services Tax applies to fees.
  • Cash withdrawal: 2.5% of the amount (at least ₹500); interest from the day of withdrawal.

Your rights

  • You can close the card at any time; the bank closes it within 7 working days of your request.
  • If you do not activate the card within 30 days, the bank asks for your consent by OTP, and otherwise closes the account at no cost.
  • If your application is declined, the bank tells you why in writing.
  • Complaints: the grievance officer, then the RBI Ombudsman if not resolved in 30 days.
